Spurs trumpet instrumental
File: spurs-trumpet.wav
New arrangement of the traditional public-domain melody When the Saints Go Marching In, also used for the Spurs chant. No vocals. Not the official Tottenham stadium recording.
Trumpet notes performed by Philharmonia Orchestra musicians; arranged and rendered for this portfolio with gentle stereo reflections.
Sample source: https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/
