Measured elevation forms the model. Surface appearance, vegetation, water and footpath are artistic additions. This is a portfolio scene, not a navigation map.

Satellite surface: Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2025). Processed by Element 84. Scene S2C_11UNS_20250823_0_L2A, 23 August 2025. Cropped, reprojected, colour graded and sharpened.
https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-l2a

Cliff material: Rock Face, Poly Haven, photography by Greg Zaal, processing by Dario Barresi; CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face
Material is mapped at artistic landscape scale and combined with authored limestone, snow and forest albedo.
